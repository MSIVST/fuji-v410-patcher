# Security and Safety Policy

## Reporting Safety Issues

If a patch causes boot loops, failed updates, audio regressions, Bluetooth regressions, map-loading issues, or other safety-relevant behavior, please open an issue and mark it clearly as a safety/regression report.

Include:

- Unit part number, if known.
- Region firmware used.
- Whether stock recovery firmware was accepted afterward.
- Exact patcher version.
- What happened on screen.
- Whether the unit recovered after removing update media or flashing stock firmware.

## Firmware and Copyright Boundaries

Do not attach Garmin/Suzuki firmware, patched firmware, extracted firmware payloads, maps, unlock files, or proprietary assets to issues or pull requests.

This repository is intended to distribute patching logic only. Users must obtain official firmware themselves.

## Warranty and Risk

This is unofficial research software. Firmware modification can brick hardware, void warranties, and produce unexpected behavior. Use at your own risk.
